# SquadSense: handoff for Claude Code

Read this first. It covers what we're building, what's decided, what's done and what comes next. Last updated 20 November 2026, when the project moved from Claude chat to Claude Code.

## What SquadSense is

A personal Fantasy Premier League (FPL) helper for Clement (FPL team ID `11304359`), with a few family members' teams added later. It runs on free data and free hosting only.

It should:

1. Recommend the best starting eleven and captain each gameweek.
2. Suggest who to buy and sell, based on form, injuries, fixture difficulty and underlying stats (xG, xA, minutes).
3. Track price changes and send alerts before deadlines and before the nightly price change.
4. Plan transfers across a 3 to 5 gameweek window and suggest when to play chips.
5. Let Clement test squad changes and budget maths before he makes them on the official FPL site. The app never makes transfers itself.

### First build (this folder)

Python data job (`jobs/`):
- `fpl_client.py`: FPL API client. One request a second, retries with backoff on 429 and 5xx, adds trailing slashes, raises `FplApiError` on bad responses.
- `rules.py`: squad shape, budget, club limit, formation rules, scoring points, selling price formula. Since 24 November 2026 each sync replaces the values FPL publishes (`game_config.scoring`, `game_settings`, `element_types`) with FPL's own through `apply_fpl_settings`, logs any difference and puts it in the snapshot as `ruleChanges`, which sends one "FPL changed its rules" alert. Past seasons and test data keep the typed-in defaults (`use_defaults`, reset before every test in `tests/conftest.py`). `POINTS_FOR_DEFENSIVE_CONTRIBUTION` is per position.
- `squad_state.py`: rebuilds bank, selling prices and banked free transfers from public endpoints.
- `fixtures.py`: club attack and concede ratings built from this season's match xG, blended with FPL's 1-5 overall rating while the sample is small (FPL's attack and defence strengths are all 0 in 2026/27); expected goals per fixture; clean-sheet chance; window scores for attackers and defenders. Where bookmaker odds exist, expected goals are a 50/50 blend with the odds' (`MARKET_WEIGHT`). Elo starting guesses (`elo.py`, `ELO_SCALE`) exist but are backtest-only, since they didn't beat the 1-5 rating.
- `odds.py`: football-data.co.uk results and odds since 2019-20 (past seasons downloaded once, this season and `fixtures.csv` every 6 hours, cached through `storage.save_raw`), margin removal, and a grid search for the Poisson expected goals that match the 1X2 and over/under 2.5 odds. `fixtures.csv` only lists the next round once football-data.co.uk switches to it midweek, so most runs before then find no odds for the planning week. If the site is down, the sync logs a warning and carries on.
- `elo.py`: our own Elo ratings from those results (K 20, home advantage 60, bigger margins move more; promoted clubs start at the relegated clubs' average). ClubElo's API was down (502, fixtures feed "deactivated"), and FPL-Core-Insights' Elo columns are empty this season for the same reason.
- `challenger.py`: `python -m squadsense.challenger`, an OpenFPL-style gradient-boosting points model (scikit-learn, dev only) scored with the backtest. It didn't beat our model on top20 or captain, so it isn't used.
- `squad_builder.py`: picks the best 15 for one gameweek with an integer programming solver (PuLP 2.x calling HiGHS). Used while a team's picks are still hidden. PuLP's bundled CBC solver is Intel-only on Macs, so it isn't used.
- `model.py`: per-player xP per gameweek (recency-weighted per-90 rates, pulled towards a starting point for small samples, scaled by fixture). The starting point is the position average moved towards his own per-90 from last season (`history_past`, up to `PAST_MINUTES_CAP` = 900 minutes; under 270 minutes is ignored). Backtested on 2026-27 GW4-10: captain 6.43 to 7.43, top20 4.84 to 5.02.
- `availability.py` (25 November 2026): FPL's `chance_of_playing_next_round` only covers the next gameweek, but it used to scale every week of the five-week plan and the chip horizon, so a one-match ban or a 50% knock cut a player's xP for five weeks (Raya, 50% with a shoulder injury, went from 11.2 to 20.3 xP over GW12-16 once fixed, after the plan had sold him). Now the next gameweek keeps the flag, and later matches use the return date in the news ("Expected back 20 Dec", "Suspended until 5 Dec", compared with each match's kick-off), one match for a dateless ban, the next week only for doubtful, and every week for an unknown return or a departed player. `PlayerRates` keeps `fit_chance_60` and `fit_chance_under_60`, and `expected_points_for_week(..., later_week=True)` scales them per match. Not done: a player back from injury still has his missed matches in his last-five minutes share, since the data can't tell injured from dropped.
- `flags.py`: injury, yellow-card ban and rotation warnings.
- `planner.py`: best eleven and captain, legal transfer checks, best single-transfer suggestions with "make it or roll" advice.
- `strategy.py` (21 November 2026): the only place chip decisions are made. Steps: the best normal-transfer plan; chip values per week against normal transfers (Wildcard and Free Hit scaled by the share of the gap normal transfers don't close); keep values (`PLAY_SHARE` 0.9 of each chip's later week, weeks shared out so no two chips collide, plus each chip's `CHIP_MARGINS`, which is 5 for the Wildcard and 0 for the rest); one solve of the five-week plan with all four chips and their keep values; each played chip's gain and reason read from the plan's own squad that week. If no chip is played, the normal-transfer plan is used as the plan so the two never differ. Decisions are play / save / none with `closeCall` within `CLOSE_CALL` 2 points. Double gameweeks (25 November 2026): `DOUBLE_WEIGHT` can weight Bench Boost's and Triple Captain's keep value toward the first known double after the five-week window. It stays 1.0 (off): in `plan_backtest` a weight of 1.2 scored 1093.7 real points per half against 1108.3, because it also moved transfers, even though the chip-only backtest liked it; `--double-weight` and `chip_backtest --double-weights` rerun it. The result is cached (`strategy/{team}/{gw}` raw) and reused until the gameweek, squad, bank, free transfers, chips used, squad injury news or `STRATEGY_VERSION` change, or it's 12 hours old: about 80-110 seconds to plan, 25 to reuse. Falls back to the simple best-week estimate (source "estimate") for a model-picked squad.
- Price timing in the plan: `price_watch.likely_price_moves` turns FPL's predictor (likelihood 3 or more, not locked) into +1 or -1 for tonight. The solver assumes the first week's moves are made now and prices later weeks after the change (buying costs £0.1m more, selling an owned player is recalculated with `rules.selling_price`). Moves it affects carry a `priceNote` ("buy now: likely to rise £0.1m tonight"). A reused plan gets its notes redone from the latest predictions on every sync (`strategy.refresh_price_notes`), so the late runs before 00:00 always have fresh notes; only the moves themselves can be up to 12 hours old.
- How sure each move is (`strategy.add_sensitivity`): the plan is re-solved `SENSITIVITY_RUNS` (15) times with noise on every xP, sized like open-fpl-solver's (xP x (92 - expected minutes) / 134 x a normal draw, seeded by gameweek), chips held where the plan put them. Each move gets `sameWeekShare` and `anyWeekShare`; the plan gets `sensitivity.firstWeekAlternatives`. Adds about a minute to a fresh plan.
- `alerts.py`: runs at the end of every sync. Builds alerts from the snapshot: a plan brief within 26 hours of the deadline and a final call within 2.5 hours (24 November 2026, `briefs.py`: transfers, captain and chips tagged FIRM, CLOSE CALL or MAKE NOW; the brief is saved as raw `brief/{team}/{gw}` and the final call lists what changed since), `priceWatch.atRisk` players the first-week plan sells, squad news and suspension flags. The per-sync "Your plan changed" alert was removed because close calls made it flip-flop. Emails are HTML with a plain-text copy (`email_template.py`), replies go to `ALERT_EMAIL_TO`. It skips keys already in `alerts_sent`, and sends by email (Resend API) and web push (`pywebpush` to every row in `push_subscriptions`, deleting expired ones on 404/410). The first run only records existing injury and ban news. With no settings it logs and sends nothing. `make_push_keys.py` makes the VAPID key pair. Website side: the bell in the header (`AccountMenu.tsx`) registers `public/sw.js`, subscribes with `NEXT_PUBLIC_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` and saves it through `/api/push`; `proxy.ts` lets the service worker, manifest and icons load without the login cookie.
- "Done on FPL" (24 November 2026): FPL hides transfers until the deadline, so a move Clement made on the FPL site is marked on the website (`MadeMoveButton.tsx` on the Plan page's first week and the Dashboard's This week card, `/api/made-moves`, `lib/madeMoves.ts`, Supabase table `made_moves`). Only first-week plan moves can be marked, at the snapshot's price; marking or undoing calls `start_sync` (granted to the server key) so a sync starts straight away. The sync applies the coming week's marks to the public squad (`squad_state.apply_made_moves`: swap, bank, purchase and selling price, one free transfer each; marks that don't fit are logged and skipped) and puts them in the snapshot as `madeMoves`; briefs list them tagged DONE. After the deadline, marks missing from the real squad go in `madeMoveMismatches` and send one alert. Locally, marks come from `jobs/.data/made_moves.json`. Since 26 November 2026 any legal transfer for the coming week can be marked, not only the plan's: the Planner's move list has the same button, `/api/made-moves` checks the move with `lib/madeMoveRules.ts` (in the squad, same position, 3 per club, enough money, counting marks saved since the last sync), the sync's `mark_fits` also checks positions, and saved drafts drop moves already made (`drafts.draftForSnapshot`).
- Deadline calendar (24 November 2026), a backup for the alerts: `web/lib/calendar.ts` builds an iCalendar feed of every deadline left (one event per gameweek from 2 hours before to the deadline, reminders at the start and 90 minutes in, fixed UID per gameweek), served at `/api/calendar/<token>` from FPL's `bootstrap-static` (cached an hour), so it doesn't need the sync. The token is an HMAC of "calendar-feed" with `SITE_SECRET` (32 hex characters); `proxy.ts` lets `/api/calendar/` through and the route checks the token. The bell menu gets the `webcal://` link from `/api/calendar-link`. `npm test` includes `tests/calendar.test.mts`. Whether iPhone Calendar keeps the reminders of a subscribed calendar hasn't been checked yet.
- `changes.py`: the "What changed" feed. Each sync stores a small summary (squad and plan players' five-week xP, price and news, first-week moves, chip decisions, captain, squad, gameweek) and compares it with the last one; anything that moved becomes a sentence. The last 20 entries are kept (`changes/{team}` raw) and shown on the Dashboard (`WhatChanged.tsx`).
- `plan_backtest.py`: `python -m squadsense.plan_backtest` replays each past half-season week by week with four managers (the full strategy via `strategy.plan_and_chips`, the plan without chips, the best single transfer, holding) from the same model-picked start and scores their real points with automatic subs. About an hour. Results (21 November 2026): strategy 1094, plan 1080, single 1016, hold 978 real points per half on average. Planning ahead clearly helps; the chip strategy adds only 14 points per half, plays all four chips in the first eight weeks of every half, and lost 2 of 6 halves; hits were heavy in two halves. Retest (22 November 2026): a 3-point margin on every chip and an extra 2-point hit cost both scored less (1082 and 1074 against 1088.5 for the live rules), so the live settings stay; they are now `strategy.CHIP_MARGINS` and `transfer_solver.HIT_PENALTY`, and `--managers` and `--season` pick what to compare. Details in `docs/backtest-results.md`.
- `chips.py` (21 November 2026 additions, now feeding strategy.py): Wildcard is now valued (best squad for the next 5 weeks from `pick_best_squad` on summed, discounted xP, minus keeping yours; this overstates it a little since normal transfers would win some back). Each card has hold-or-play advice ("play" when this week is worth at least `PLAY_SHARE` = 0.9 of the best week left, chosen by `chip_backtest.py`), risk numbers (Triple Captain haul and blank chance from `captaincy.py`, Bench Boost chance all four bench play, Wildcard number of changes), a gain for every week of the half, and "near"/"far" estimate quality (beyond the next five gameweeks is "far"). The plan also carries `fixtureWarnings` (known blanks and doubles, matches with no gameweek, provisional kick-offs), `withTransfers` (chip weeks from the five-week transfer plan) and `rivals` (chips each mini-league rival has left, from their cached entry history).
- `chip_backtest.py`: `python -m squadsense.chip_backtest` replays each half of the downloaded seasons with a model-picked squad kept all half and compares chip timing methods. Results in `docs/backtest-results.md`.
- `chips.py`: Chip Planner. Chip windows come from bootstrap `chips` (each chip twice: GW1-19 and GW20-38). For each week left in the current half it values Bench Boost (bench xP), Triple Captain (captain xP once more) and Free Hit (best one-week squad within budget, via `squad_builder`, minus your lineup), then tries every week combination so no two chips share a gameweek. Wildcard is valued too (see the additions above). Free Hit and Wildcard are skipped in a team's first week, when transfers are unlimited.
- `past_seasons.py`: loads downloaded past seasons (vaastav dataset, in `jobs/.data/past/<season>/`) into live-API shapes. Maps the old `strength` column (1-5) onto `strength_overall_*` and skips 2024-25 assistant manager rows.
- `backtest.py`: `python -m squadsense.backtest` predicts each gameweek from earlier matches only and scores starters (top20, captain, slope, mae, bias per position, and bias by real points: blank, return, big, haul). `--elo` and `--odds` switch those inputs on, `--horizon 3` also scores predictions made 2 and 3 weeks ahead, and `--tune` runs a coordinate search. Results are in `docs/backtest-results.md`.
- Goals conceded (`model.expected_goals_conceded_penalty`): FPL takes 1 point per 2 goals, rounded down, so the model now adds that up over a Poisson spread instead of using goals/2. It removed most of this season's defender under-rating (-0.24 to -0.04 per start). A Dixon-Coles scoreline correction was looked at and skipped: it changes joint scorelines but leaves each side's clean-sheet chance exactly the same.
- `player_detail.py`: one detail entry per player (header, last 6 gameweeks with doubles added together, per-90 rates, next 6 fixtures rated like the ticker, price status). Stored in the snapshot as `playerDetails`.
- `price_watch.py`: Price Watch rows from FPL's own predictor fields (progress, next-change projection and its -5 to 5 likelihood, price locks), sorted by progress, plus the squad's at-risk and rising players (likelihood 3 or more). The likelihood bands were read from live data, not documentation.
- `ticker.py`: Fixture Ticker data for the next 8 gameweeks: each match's attack and defence difficulty (1 easiest to 5 hardest, split into five equal groups across all matches shown) and window scores for 3, 5 and 8 weeks.
- `captaincy.py`: plays each captain option's week 4,000 times with random goals, assists and clean sheets from the model's rates (seeded per player) and gives `haulChance` (10+ points) and `blankChance` (2 or fewer). The bonus guess inside it is rough. `captain_calibration.py` (22 November 2026) checked both chances on 560 past captain options: the haul chance was too confident (options given over 60% hauled about 30% of the time). Since 24 November 2026 both chances are corrected with Platt scaling (`calibrate`, `HAUL_CALIBRATION`, `BLANK_CALIBRATION`), fitted on those options and checked season by season against seasons left out of the fit; `simulated_chances` gives the raw numbers. See `docs/backtest-results.md`.
- `transfer_solver.py`: the five-week transfer plan (`solverPlan`). One integer problem over every week at once (PuLP plus HiGHS, 30-second limit): squad flow with buys and sells, bank, free transfers banking to 5 (a yes/no `carries_any` stops fake banking through extra hits), -4 hits, a legal eleven and captain each week. The pool is the squad plus the top 12 per position. It plans all four chips: Bench Boost, Triple Captain, Wildcard (no hits that week, banked transfers kept) and Free Hit (the best one-week squad for each week is worked out first; in that week it scores instead of yours, your squad can't transfer, and banked transfers are kept), plus a keep value per chip. `SOLVER_GAP` is 0.2% and `SOLVER_SECONDS` 300: at 1% the chip choice flipped between runs on the same data. The Planner page has a chip picker per week and "Load into planner" loads the plan's chips too (`simulatePlan` in `planning.ts` handles them). Later weeks are discounted with `planner.WEEKLY_DISCOUNT`; `END_FREE_TRANSFER_VALUE` (1.0) and `MAX_TRANSFERS_PER_WEEK` (3) are untested guesses. Skipped for a model-picked squad.
- `leagues.py`: lists every private league you're in (or only the ids in `SQUADSENSE_LEAGUES`), smallest first. Leagues of up to `FULL_ANALYSIS_MAX` (100) teams are "full": every page of standings and each rival's picks and chip history for the last deadline (cached per team and gameweek through `storage.load_raw`), giving the table, rival ownership and captaincy, and "This week's exposure" (26 November 2026, replacing the old threats and differentials; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-pre-deadline-exposure-design.md`): `league_exposure` compares rivals' last lineups (captain 2, starter 1, bench 0; a Triple Captain or Bench Boost read as a normal week; Free Hit rivals left out) with your planned eleven and captain for the coming week, and gives each player's effective ownership, yours, the share of rivals who own and captain him, and his swing ((yours minus theirs) times his xP), with "Costing you" and "Gaining you" lists of 6 and the total swing. Big leagues split it into Top 10 and Around you (`exposure_groups`, sharing `sample_groups` with Live); `ExposureCard.tsx` shows it. Bigger leagues are "table": the top 50 plus the 50 around your rank, saved as `standings/{league}` and refetched once a day; since 24 November 2026 they also read the picks of a sample (`sample_entries`: the top `SAMPLE_TOP` 10 and the `SAMPLE_AROUND` 10 either side of you, no chip histories), so their exposure and captains come from the sample (`picksFrom` "sample" against "all"). They still have no chip rows or Live totals. Every summary has `standing` (gap to the leader and the teams above and below). The Leagues page shows 20 teams a page (`lib/leagueTable.ts`, Previous, Next, Jump to me, opening on your page), Where you stand, and the three cards for every league. The first sync after a deadline reads about 400 rivals (about 14 minutes), so the workflow timeout is 45 minutes. The Leagues page picks a league from a dropdown (22 November 2026).
- `review.py` (25 November 2026 changes): the reviewed week's points come from FPL's `event/{gw}/live/` (`live_week_points`, saved once as raw `final_points/{gw}` by `sync.reviewed_week_points`), falling back to saved histories; the team's week splits starters and bench by FPL position (`lineup_split`), so a Bench Boost bench is no longer counted as starters or as a captain option, and carries `bench` and `viceCaptain`; `TOP_PLAYERS_SHOWN` is 8.
- `review.py`: each sync saves a forecast for the coming week (lineup, captain, each squad player's xP) as raw `forecast/{team}/{gw}`. Once that week's points are final, the review shows points, rank move, bench points, hits, captain against the best captain in the eleven and xP against points per starter, plus the FPL average, highest, most captained and top 5 players. Teams that hadn't started get the league-wide part only.
- `snapshot.py`: runs the model and packs everything the website needs into one JSON snapshot. Also carries `livePicks` (every team's picks for the week being played, plus each player's club and position) for the Live page; `getSnapshot()` strips it like `playerDetails`.
- `storage.py`: the same methods for local JSON files or Supabase, including past seasons (`career/` folder or the `player_past` table) and `load_raw` for cached responses.
- `sync.py`: the job's entry point (`python -m squadsense.sync`).
- `tests/`: 27 pytest tests and a made-up FPL dataset (`sample_data.py`) used by the tests and the website's demo mode. The sample mirrors the 2026/27 API shape (zeroed strengths, `fixture` and `was_home` on history rows).

Database: `db/schema.sql` creates the tables (`api_cache`, `player_history`, `predictions`, `price_snapshots`, `snapshots`) with row level security on.

## Known gaps and assumptions

- Free transfers after a Wildcard or Free Hit: the code assumes you keep the banked ones and still gain +1 the next week (about 70% confident). Toggle `GAIN_TRANSFER_ON_CHIP_WEEKS` in `squad_state.py`.
- Model v2 (20 November 2026): `ASSIST_FACTOR` 1.4 and forward bonus prior 0.6, both measured from data. Other `model.py` weights were backtested and kept, since nothing beat them by more than noise. Still untested: `PRIOR_GAMES` and `RATING_STEP` in `fixtures.py`, `BENCH_WEIGHT` in `squad_builder.py`, and `roll_value` in `planner.py`.
- Defenders are under-rated by about 0.3 points per start this season (the first with defensive contributions). Rerun the backtest after a few more gameweeks before acting on it.
- Players kept from the original squad get their purchase price from their first match in the team's first gameweek (`started_event`), which can be £0.1m off.
- Clement's 2026/27 team was created on 20 November 2026 and starts in GW11. Its squad isn't public until the GW11 deadline (21 November, 11:00 UTC), so `sync` fails with a 404 on `picks` before then. Free transfers and starting prices now count from `started_event`.
- Transfers made for a gameweek whose deadline hasn't passed aren't visible in public data.
- The single-transfer suggestions only test one move; the five-week plan covers pairs and rolls. Neither considers chips.
- Rival names from mini-league standings are stored in the snapshot, so anyone who can open the hosted site can read them.
- FPL doesn't publish every rule: the 4-point hit, the defensive contribution thresholds and the 1 point per 2 goals conceded and per 3 saves stay typed in `rules.py`. The website's Planner keeps its own copy of the squad rules in `web/lib/planning.ts`; the "FPL changed its rules" alert says when to check it.
- The repo is public since 24 November 2026 (Clement's choice, for unlimited Actions minutes: runs average 4.1 minutes, about 1,600 a month, too close to a private repo's 2,000 for family teams). ## Things to watch out for

- Prices are stored in tenths of a million everywhere (145 means £14.5m). Only `web/lib/format.ts` turns them into "£14.5m".
- Rules exist twice, in `jobs/squadsense/rules.py` and `planner.py` and in `web/lib/planning.ts`. Change both together.
- The snapshot's shape is defined by `jobs/squadsense/snapshot.py` and typed in `web/lib/types.ts`. Change both together.
- After changing the model, regenerate the demo data with `python -m tests.make_demo_snapshot` (from `jobs/`).
- Every FPL path needs a trailing slash. Keep requests at one a second or slower.
- Never commit `.env`, `.env.local` or the Supabase service role key. `.gitignore` already covers them. `web/.env.local` also holds `SITE_PASSWORD`, `SITE_SECRET` and, if Clement adds them, `FPL_EMAIL` and `FPL_PASSWORD`.
- Apply to FPL (`web/lib/fplAccount.ts`, `web/lib/applyPlan.ts`, `web/app/api/apply/route.ts`, `web/components/ApplyToFpl.tsx`): only localhost, only with FPL credentials set, only the next gameweek. Apply repeats the checks, needs the typed word APPLY, then posts `/api/transfers/` (with `wildcard`/`freehit` flags, `confirmed: false` as AIrsenal does) and `/api/my-team/{id}/` (lineup, captain, and `chip` for Bench Boost or Triple Captain, which AIrsenal says only work on the website, so the UI warns). `npm test` in `web/` runs 12 checks on the payload builder.
