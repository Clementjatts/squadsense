# This week's exposure: effective ownership before the deadline

Approved by Clement on 26 November 2026. He asked that nothing about his
opponents be lost, and left the layout to Claude.

## Why

The Leagues page showed "Threats" (players 30% or more of rivals own and
Clement doesn't) and "Differentials" (his players 25% or fewer own). Both
ignore captains and benches, so a player every rival captains counted the
same as one they all bench. Effective ownership counts both, which is what
decides who gains points on whom.

## Approaches

- Replace Threats and Differentials with one exposure card (chosen). Every
  player the old cards listed still appears, with the same ownership and
  captaincy numbers, plus effective ownership and the expected swing.
- Keep both and add the new card (rejected): two slightly different answers
  to the same question on one page.

## What gets built

One "This week's exposure" card per league on the Leagues page:

- A headline: points Clement's planned team is expected to gain or lose
  against the average rival this week (sum of every player's swing).
- "Costing you": players rivals own more than he does, biggest expected
  loss first. "Gaining you": his players the league owns less, biggest
  expected gain first. Six each.
- Each player shows: rivals who own him, rivals who captain him, rivals'
  effective ownership (starting counts once, captain twice, bench zero),
  Clement's own, and the swing: (his minus theirs) times the player's xP
  for the coming week.
- His side is the plan's best eleven and captain for the coming week.
- Rivals' sides are their last lineups (their new picks are hidden until the
  deadline), labelled as such. A Triple Captain counts as a normal captain
  and a Bench Boost bench as benched, since those chips are one-off; a
  rival who played Free Hit is left out, since that squad is gone.
- Big leagues get the same Top 10 / Around you switch as the Live page,
  from the sample the sync already reads.
- "Rivals' captains" stays.

## Left to the builder

Card layout within the page's rows-that-end-together rule.
